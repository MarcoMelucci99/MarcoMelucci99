## 🧑‍💻 About Me

Security Transformation Analyst at **Accenture Cybersecurity**, focused on **OT/ICS** and **Automotive** security: I help industrial and automotive clients protect the systems that run in the physical world, from plant floors to connected vehicles.

My background mixes tech and social sciences: I've researched how digitalization reshapes society and the labor market, and outside work I'm still the one building (and breaking) PCs, following tech & gaming news, and grinding labs on TryHackMe and Hack The Box.

> *Curiosity drives progress.* 🔍
